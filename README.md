# 🛍️ Heartix — Premium Smart Appliances

Heartix is a full-featured e-commerce website for smart appliances, built with modern web technologies and Firebase.

**Live Site:** https://heartix.vercel.app

---

## ✨ Features

### 🛒 Customer Features
- ✅ Premium responsive UI (mobile-first)
- ✅ Product browsing with categories
- ✅ Advanced search with auto-suggestions
- ✅ Wishlist (local, ready for cloud sync)
- ✅ Shopping cart with stock validation
- ✅ Coupon code support
- ✅ Secure checkout with transaction
- ✅ Multiple payment methods (COD, Delivery Advance, Full Online)
- ✅ Order tracking
- ✅ Customer account (Login/Register)
- ✅ Product reviews & ratings
- ✅ Recently viewed products
- ✅ Contact form

### 👑 Admin Features
- ✅ Secure admin panel (Firebase Auth)
- ✅ Product management (add/edit/delete)
- ✅ Category management with images
- ✅ Order management (edit, status change)
- ✅ Coupon creation & management
- ✅ Banner/section management
- ✅ Customer reviews management
- ✅ Contact messages inbox
- ✅ CSV export for orders
- ✅ Dashboard with stats

---

| Component | Technology |
